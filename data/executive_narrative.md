# NexaTech Industries — Executive Financial Narrative

**Period:** Trailing 3 months ending Aug 2026

## Headline KPIs

- Revenue: ₹255.32 Cr (Growth: -4.0%)
- Budget Variance: -7.34%
- EBITDA: ₹32.17 Cr (Margin: 12.6%)
- Gross Margin: 49.68%
- DSO: 57.2 days | DPO: 38.5 days | Cash Conversion Cycle: 25.9 days

## Total Revenue vs Budget

**Finding:** Company revenue is -7.3% below budget over the trailing 3 months.

**Root cause chain:**
- Region driver: North region is the largest negative contributor, running -14.5% below its budget.
- Product driver: within North, Product B revenue is down -8.3% versus the prior 3-month period.
- Root cause: average discount on Product B in North has increased by 2.0 percentage points over the same window, consistent with a pricing/competitive response eroding realized revenue.

**Business impact:** At the current run rate, sustaining this discount level costs approximately ₹5.6 Cr in foregone revenue over 3 months for Product B in North alone, and is the single largest driver of the company-wide budget miss.

**Recommendation:** Freeze further discount escalation on Product B in North; run a win/loss review on recent deals to confirm whether the competitive threat is real or self-inflicted, and reallocate sales incentives toward higher-margin, on-plan products in that region while a pricing review is completed.

## Cash Conversion Cycle

**Finding:** Company-wide cash conversion cycle has moved from 23 to 25 days, a deterioration of 2 days.

**Root cause chain:**
- Region driver: East shows the sharpest DSO increase, up 4 days versus the prior period.
- Root cause: extended payment terms granted to large enterprise accounts in East are slowing collections faster than payables or inventory are improving.

**Business impact:** Every additional day of DSO ties up roughly ₹0.66 Cr of working capital in East alone, directly reducing free cash available for reinvestment.

**Recommendation:** Renegotiate payment terms on the top 3-5 overdue accounts in East, introduce early-payment incentives, and set a DSO ceiling as a regional finance KPI going forward.
